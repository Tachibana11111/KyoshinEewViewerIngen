## About the estimated shaking arrival circles

They are displayed by linearly interpolating the JMA2001 travel-time table.  
Because of the travel-time table, circles are not displayed when the estimated radius is 2000 km or more.

## About earthquake information

### [JMA Disaster Prevention XML (PULL type)](https://xml.kishou.go.jp/xmlpull.html)

Although slower than tools that scrape other sites,  
this is a reliable method that is less affected by specification changes.

### [Project DM-D.S.S](https://dmdata.jp/)

Although it is paid, it is currently the fastest way for individuals to obtain telegrams.
