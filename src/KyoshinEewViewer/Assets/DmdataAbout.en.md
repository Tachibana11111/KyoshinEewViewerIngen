## About [Project DM-D.S.S](https://dmdata.jp/)

This is a service that delivers earthquake information and more in real time using WebSocket and other methods. (paid)  
By default, KyoshinEewViewer for ingen receives earthquake information using the [JMA Disaster Prevention XML (PULL type)](http://xml.kishou.go.jp/xmlpull.html), but this method introduces a lag of **about 1 to 3 minutes** from announcement to reception.  
Using DM-D.S.S reduces it to **a few seconds**, allowing you to receive information at a speed equal to or faster than TV and apps provided by companies.

> [!WARNING]
> Under the individual plan contract, it is prohibited to provide an API that publicly distributes the content of Earthquake Early Warnings (forecast) received from DM-D.S.S, or to post it to social media with a bot.

If you integrate it with workflows and so on, please be especially careful.
[Please check here for details](https://dmdata.jp/docs/eew#%E5%86%8D%E9%85%8D%E4%BF%A1%E3%83%9D%E3%83%AA%E3%82%B7%E3%83%BC).
