## About [VOICEVOX](https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/)

VOICEVOX is a free, medium-quality text-to-speech and singing voice synthesis software.

KyoshinEewViewer for ingen can announce the content of triggers by voice as an action of the workflow feature.  
By default, it becomes available as an action once you start and enable VOICEVOX on the same PC.

VOICEVOX only performs the synthesis, so the audio itself is played by KyoshinEewViewer for ingen.  
Please note that it may take a little time (several seconds or more) before the audio is actually played because of the speech synthesis.
